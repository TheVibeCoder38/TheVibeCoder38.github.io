Privacy Policy

Car Crossing — Traffic Puzzle
Effective Date: 26 November 2026
Developer: The Vibe Coder

The Vibe Coder ("we", "our", or "us") operates Car Crossing — Traffic Puzzle ("the App"). This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the App.

We aim to collect as little information as possible. The App does not require you to create an account or provide your name, email address, phone number, or other personal details to play.

1. Information Stored on Your Device

The App stores certain gameplay and preference information locally on your device, including:

  • Game progress, unlocked levels, scores and completion times.
  • Game settings, such as audio, haptics and graphics preferences.
  • Achievements, selected vehicle skins and cosmetic items.
  • Virtual coin balances and reward progress.
  • Daily challenge, mission and reward progress.
  • Local gameplay statistics, such as level attempts and collision counts.
  • Advertising and privacy consent preferences.

This information is used to operate the game and remember your progress. Our audit found no app backend or cloud account system receiving this gameplay information.

Android device backup may include some locally stored app data if Android Backup is enabled on your device.

2. Advertising and Google AdMob

The App uses Google AdMob, a third-party advertising service provided by Google, to display advertisements.

When advertisements are requested or displayed, Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect or process information such as:

  • Advertising ID and other device identifiers.
  • IP address, which may be used to infer approximate location.
  • Advertisement impressions, clicks and interactions.
  • Advertising performance, diagnostics and related technical information.

This information may be used for advertising, ad measurement, fraud prevention, security and compliance, and related services.

Google may process information in accordance with its own privacy policies. For more information, visit Google's Privacy Policy .

3. Advertising Consent

The App integrates Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage applicable advertising consent requirements.

Where required, you may be shown a consent message and may be able to manage your advertising privacy choices through the App's privacy options.

Your choices may affect whether personalized or non-personalized advertisements are requested. Availability of particular privacy options depends on your location and applicable requirements.

4. Google Fonts

The App uses Google Fonts. When your device connects to Google's font services to retrieve font resources, Google may receive standard network information such as your IP address and browser or device user-agent information.

Google's privacy information is available at https://policies.google.com/privacy .

5. Analytics and Crash Reporting

Our current app implementation does not include Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, Google Analytics, or third-party analytics and crash-reporting SDKs.

The App may maintain gameplay statistics locally on your device. These statistics are not transmitted to our own server. Google's advertising SDK may process advertising-related diagnostics and performance information as described above.

6. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

We do not operate our own user-account service, cloud-save service, or advertising-data database.

Information may be processed by Google through its advertising, consent-management and font services as described in this policy. Google's handling of that information is governed by Google's own policies and applicable terms.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Gameplay information and preferences are stored locally on your device. You can generally remove locally stored App data by clearing the App's storage or uninstalling the App.

Some data may also be included in Android device backups if backup is enabled.

Information processed by Google is subject to Google's own retention and deletion practices.

8. Children's Privacy

Car Crossing — Traffic Puzzle is intended for a general audience and is not specifically designed or directed to children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children through our own account or registration system. The App's advertising and audience settings are intended to be configured in accordance with applicable laws and Google Play policies.

If you believe that information has been handled improperly, please contact us using the details below.

9. Data Security

We use reasonable measures within the App to protect communications. The current Android configuration disables cleartext network traffic.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Third-Party Services

The App currently integrates the following Google services:

  • Google AdMob — advertising.
  • Google User Messaging Platform — advertising consent management.
  • Google Fonts — font delivery.

These services may process information under their own privacy policies and terms.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App's features, services, or legal requirements change. The updated policy will be published at this same URL with a revised effective date.
